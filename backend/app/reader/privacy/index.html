Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Edulens360 website at https://edulens360.com/ and the Edulens360 Android app (package name com.edulens360.app).

Who we are

Edulens360 is a Ghana education news reader. It shows short summaries of education stories and links you to the original publisher for the full report.

What the app and site do

The Android app opens the Edulens360 website in an in-app browser (WebView). There is no account sign-in. You do not create a user profile with us.

Information we collect

Information stored on your device

  • Saved stories and pinned categories may be stored locally on your device (for example in browser storage) so those choices stay available when you return.
  • That information stays on your device. We do not operate a login system that uploads those saves to our servers.

Technical information needed to show the feed

  • When you open Edulens360, your device requests pages, images, and the news feed over the internet. Standard web request details (such as IP address, device or browser type, and the page requested) can be processed by the hosting provider that serves the site.
  • The Android app uses the internet permission only to load Edulens360 and linked pages.

Information we do not collect through the app

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details in the app.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs in the current Android app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

How we use information

We use technical request data only to:

  • deliver the website and Android app experience
  • keep the service working, secure, and available
  • understand basic traffic so we can improve reliability

Sharing with other parties

  • News publishers. When you tap a source link, you leave Edulens360 and visit that publisher’s site or app. Their own privacy policy then applies.
  • Hosting. The public site is hosted on infrastructure that may process standard server logs (currently GitHub Pages for the public website).
  • Google Play. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may process install and device information under Google’s own policies.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Edulens360 is a general education-news reader. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

Saved stories and pinned categories remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app or browser storage. Hosting logs, if kept by the provider, follow that provider’s retention practices.

Your choices

  • Remove a saved story or pinned category in the app or site.
  • Clear site data or uninstall the Android app to remove local storage.
  • Stop using Edulens360 at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app or site changes. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions about Edulens360, use the contact email shown on the Edulens360 Google Play store listing, or write to us through the support contact published with the app.
