GMA, WiPR Ghana mark International Day of the Girl Child with literacy initiative

Global Media Alliance (GMA) and Women in PR Ghana (WiPR Ghana) have empowered more than 300 girls from Dzorwulu A & B Primary School and Dzorwulu Junior High School through the third edition of their Read4Her Future literacy and mentorship initiative, held to mark the 2026 International Day of the Girl Child.
