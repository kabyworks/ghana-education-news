Keta Senior High Technical School (KETASCO) were crowned champions in the girls' and boys’ categories of the Volta Region version of the KGL Mental-Slam basketball competition. The KETASCO boys beat the hosts, Mawuli School, by just one point in a thrilling final to lift the trophy and claim the bragging rights in the four-school tournament. The game ended 22–21 in favour of KETASCO. They kicked off the competition by securing 25 points in a 42-point match against their compatriots from Awudome Senior High School, booking a place in the final.
