NACOC educates 450 Koforidua SHS students on dangers of drug abuse

The Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) has educated about 450 first-year students of Ghana Senior High School in Koforidua on the dangers of substance abuse and its effects on their health, academic performance and social development.
