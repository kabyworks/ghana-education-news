Parliamentary Human Rights Committee urges CID, Attorney-General to expedite Obuasi SHTS abuse case

The Parliamentary Committee on Human Rights has called on the Criminal Investigations Department (CID) and the Attorney-General to expedite investigations and prosecutorial processes into allegations of sexual abuse involving the headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS).
