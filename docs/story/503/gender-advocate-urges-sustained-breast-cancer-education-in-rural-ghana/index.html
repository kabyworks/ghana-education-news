Gender advocate urges sustained breast cancer education in rural Ghana

Ghanaian media personality and US-based healthcare professional, Josephine Oppong-Yeboah has urged the Ghana Health Service (GHS) to intensify breast cancer education and outreach programmes, particularly among women in rural communities.
