Kpandai MP supports 166 tertiary students with GH¢166,000 from Common Fund

The Member of Parliament for Kpandai in the Northern Region Mathew Nyindam has supported 166 tertiary students in the constituency with a total amount of GH¢166,000 from his MP’s Common Fund. Each beneficiary received a cheque to support their education and ease the financial burden on their families.
