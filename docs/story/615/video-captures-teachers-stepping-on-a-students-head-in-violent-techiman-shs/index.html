A disturbing video circulating on social media has revealed a violent incident at Techiman Senior High School (SHS) in the Bono East Region, showing two teachers assaulting a final-year student. The video footage, which has sparked outrage, shows the two men assaulting the student, including stepping on the student's head. The teachers involved have been identified as Mr Jerry Jones Owusu Bekoe and Mr Adusa Ameyaw.
