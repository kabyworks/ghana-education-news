NDC Deputy National Organizer Aspirant Hamza Umar targets grassroots mobilisation

Businessman and former student leader Hamza Umar has declared his intention to contest for Deputy National Organizer of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), pledging to strengthen grassroots participation and coordination within the party.

MyJoyOnline Education

Businessman and former student leader Hamza Umar has declared his intention to contest for Deputy National Organizer of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), pledging to strengthen grassroots participation and coordination within the party. Mr Umar, an activist from Tafo in the Ashanti Region, says his ambition is to improve collaboration between the party’s national, regional and constituency structures while ensuring that grassroots members have a stronger voice in decision-making. He previously served as President of the Local National Union of Ghana Students at the University for Development Studies (UDS) and has participated in constituency and regional campaigns within the NDC. He also contested for the position of Constituency Deputy Organizer in Tafo and currently serves on the constituency’s Financial Committee.
