More than 80 deaf students in Savelugu receive practical robotics training by Untamed voices

More than 80 students of the Savelugu School for the Deaf in the Northern Region have received practical training in robotics and basic electronics as part of an initiative to expand access to Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) education for Deaf and hard-of-hearing young people.
