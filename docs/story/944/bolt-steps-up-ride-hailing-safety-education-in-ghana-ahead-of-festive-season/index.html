Ride-hailing company Bolt has intensified its safety education campaign in Ghana, highlighting driver vetting, passenger verification and shared responsibility between drivers and passengers. The company outlined its prevention-first approach at a Safety Experience Workshop in Accra on Wednesday, November 18, 2026. The event brought together representatives of the National Road Safety Authority (NRSA), passengers, driver-partners and the media. Bolt said its safety measures begin before a driver accepts a trip, with identity verification and checks on driver and vehicle documentation forming part of the onboarding process. Passengers are also encouraged to take precautions before entering a vehicle by checking the driver and vehicle details displayed on the app to ensure they match the information provided for their trip. Other in-app safeguards include Pickup Codes, which help passengers confirm they are entering the correct vehicle, and voluntary Rider Verification.
