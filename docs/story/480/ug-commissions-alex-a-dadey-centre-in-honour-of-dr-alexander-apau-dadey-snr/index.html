UG commissions Alex A. Dadey Centre in honour of Dr Alexander Apau Dadey Snr, Father of KGL Boss

The University of Ghana (UG) has commissioned the Alex A. Dadey Centre at its Legon campus in honour of Dr Alexander Apau Dadey Snr., a veteran educationist and administrator whose career spanned teaching, institutional leadership and public service. The facility was commissioned on Friday, October 23, 2026, in a partnership between the University and the University of Ghana Alumni Association (UGAA). To ensure its long-term sustainability, the KGL Foundation has committed to funding the centre for the next 20 years. Speaking at the ceremony, the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Ghana, Professor Nana Aba Appiah Amfo, said Dr Dadey Snr. was a former student of the University who later served on its governing council from 1978 to 1985.
