The University of Ghana’s 2026/2027 academic calendar could be severely disrupted if the ongoing strike by the Ghana Association of University Administrators (GAUA) extends into next week, Pro-Vice-Chancellor Professor Gordon Awandare has warned. Prof. Awandare said several critical university systems are currently not functioning because they are managed by GAUA members who are participating in the industrial action. “The reality is that currently many of the systems are not working because they are managed by members of GAUA who are on strike,” he said. “So the university can only do so much in terms of what we can make available in the absence of GAUA people.”
