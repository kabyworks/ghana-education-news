KGL Foundation commits $2m to maintain Mfantsipim’s Alex Dadey Sports Centre

The KGL Foundation has committed an additional $2 million towards the long-term maintenance of the Alex Dadey Sports Centre at Mfantsipim School, following its commissioning as part of the school's 150th anniversary celebrations.

MyJoyOnline Education

The KGL Foundation has committed an additional $2 million towards the long-term maintenance of the Alex Dadey Sports Centre at Mfantsipim School, following its commissioning as part of the school's 150th anniversary celebrations. The commitment, announced by the chairman of the anniversary celebration and KGL Group founder, Alex Apau Dadey, will provide the facility with an annual maintenance grant of $100,000 over the next 20 years. Speaking at the anniversary event, Mr Dadey said the funding was intended to ensure that the sports centre remained functional and continued to serve generations of students. He described the facility as an investment in sporting excellence and the school's future, stressing that the value of infrastructure depended not only on its construction but also on how well it was maintained. “The true measure of infrastructure investment is not the grandeur of its commissioning, but the quality of its maintenance and the longevity of its service,” he said.
