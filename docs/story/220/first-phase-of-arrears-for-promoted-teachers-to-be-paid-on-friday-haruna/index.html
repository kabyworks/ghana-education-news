First phase of arrears for promoted teachers to be paid on Friday – Haruna Iddrisu announces

The Minister of Education, Haruna Iddrisu, has announced that the government would honour its promise to pay promoted teachers their due salaries by the end of October 2026. He indicated that the first phase of payment would be made by Friday the 16th, based on the new grades of the promoted teachers.
