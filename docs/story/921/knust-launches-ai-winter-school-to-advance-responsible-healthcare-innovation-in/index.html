The Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) has launched a winter school focused on developing responsible, African-led artificial intelligence solutions for healthcare, with researchers already testing AI tools to support stroke diagnosis using brain scans from Ghanaian patients. The RAIL-DATAIA Winter School, organised by the university’s Responsible Artificial Intelligence Lab (RAIL), brings together researchers, students, health professionals and international partners from Ghana and France to explore practical applications of AI in healthcare. The programme forms part of the Africa Forward AI Clusters initiative, a collaboration between African universities and French institutions aimed at strengthening AI research, training and entrepreneurship across the continent.
