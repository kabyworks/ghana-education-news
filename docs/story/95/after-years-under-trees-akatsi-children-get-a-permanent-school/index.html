After years under trees, Akatsi children get a permanent school

For years, children at Akatsi Islamic Primary School in Ghana’s Volta Region had no permanent classrooms to call their own. They studied under trees and moved between unfinished temporary structures as the community struggled with inadequate educational facilities.
