Ensign Global University and the University of Utah have graduated 20 entrepreneurs from the second cohort of the Master of Business Creation (MBC) programme, an initiative designed to help business owners strengthen their enterprises and create employment opportunities. The programme takes a practical approach to entrepreneurship education, allowing participants to apply lessons in areas such as marketing, financial projections and human resource management directly to their businesses. President of Ensign Global University, Professor Stephen Alder, said the initiative could help address Africa’s employment challenges by equipping graduates to create jobs rather than rely solely on existing vacancies.
