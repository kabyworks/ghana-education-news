Assisi Girls SHS launches 70th anniversary as Joyce Bawah Mogtari, stakeholders champion girl-child education

Hundreds of alumnae, traditional authorities, educators, and students converged on the campus of St. Francis of Assisi Girls’ Senior High School in Jirapa on Saturday, October 17, to mark the institution’s 67th birthday, launch its upcoming 70th anniversary celebrations, and hold its annual Speech and Prize Giving Day.
