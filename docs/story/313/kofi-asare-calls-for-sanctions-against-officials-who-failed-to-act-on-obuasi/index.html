Kofi Asare calls for sanctions against officials who failed to act on Obuasi SHTS abuse allegations

The Executive Director of Africa Education Watch, Kofi Asare, has called for an assessment of the conduct of public officials who may have failed to act on reports of alleged sexual abuse at Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), saying accountability must extend beyond the headmaster facing criminal charges.
