Caveman Watches partners with Mfantsipim School for 150th anniversary

Ghanaian watch brand Caveman Watches has partnered with Mfantsipim School to mark the institution’s 150th anniversary, celebrating its history of academic excellence, leadership and service.

MyJoyOnline Education

Ghanaian watch brand Caveman Watches has partnered with Mfantsipim School to mark the institution’s 150th anniversary, celebrating its history of academic excellence, leadership and service. The partnership brings together the Accra-based watchmaker and the Cape Coast school, which was established in 1876 and is marking 150 years of educating generations of students who have contributed to Ghana and beyond. In a press release dated October 14, 2026, Caveman Watches said the collaboration reflects a shared appreciation for heritage, precision, excellence and lasting impact.
