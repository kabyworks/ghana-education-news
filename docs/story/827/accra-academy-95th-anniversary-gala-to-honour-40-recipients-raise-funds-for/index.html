Accra Academy 95th anniversary gala to honour 40 recipients, raise funds for school projects

Accra Academy will honour 40 individuals, groups and organisations at its 95th anniversary dinner, awards and fundraising gala on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The event, scheduled for 7:00 p.m.

MyJoyOnline Education

Accra Academy will honour 40 individuals, groups and organisations at its 95th anniversary dinner, awards and fundraising gala on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The event, scheduled for 7:00 p.m. at the Ridge Church Main Auditorium in Accra, will be held under the theme, “A Legacy of Pride, Purpose, and Achievements”. A major part of the celebration will be a fundraising drive to support the construction of residential flats for school staff and the refurbishment of the K.G. Konuah Assembly Hall. The school says the projects are intended to improve staff welfare and provide a more conducive environment for teachers living on campus.
