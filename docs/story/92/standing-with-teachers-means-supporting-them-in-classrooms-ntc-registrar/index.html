‘Standing with teachers means supporting them in classrooms’ – NTC Registrar

The Registrar of the National Teaching Council (NTC), Dr Christian Addai-Poku, has called for greater recognition and sustained support for teachers, stressing their critical role in shaping Ghana’s future.

MyJoyOnline Education

The Registrar of the National Teaching Council (NTC), Dr Christian Addai-Poku, has called for greater recognition and sustained support for teachers, stressing their critical role in shaping Ghana’s future. He made the call at the Ghana Teacher Prize 2026 Grand Durbar, held to celebrate teachers for their contribution to education and national development. Dr Addai-Poku said teachers’ responsibilities extend beyond classroom instruction, as they help learners develop the knowledge, values and confidence needed to navigate life.
