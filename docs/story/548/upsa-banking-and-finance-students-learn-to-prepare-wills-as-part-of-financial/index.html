UPSA Banking and Finance students learn to prepare wills as part of financial literacy drive

Level 400 Banking and Finance students at the University of Professional Studies, Accra (UPSA), have been guided to write their first wills as part of efforts to promote early estate planning and responsible wealth management among young people.
