Accra Metropolitan University postpones Dzenten Festival amid Tema-Nungua land dispute

Accra Metropolitan University has postponed the Dzenten Festival, scheduled for Sunday, November 1, 2026, citing security concerns arising from a dispute between the traditional authorities of Tema and Nungua over ownership of land and the Meridian Rock.

Accra Metropolitan University has postponed the Dzenten Festival, scheduled for Sunday, November 1, 2026, citing security concerns arising from a dispute between the traditional authorities of Tema and Nungua over ownership of land and the Meridian Rock. In a statement issued on Thursday, October 29, signed by Mr Ebenezer T. Hanson, Head of Public Relations and Communications at Accra Metropolitan University, the University said the decision was taken as a precautionary measure until the concerns surrounding the dispute had been addressed.
