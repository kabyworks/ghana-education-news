Police explain why only one Obuasi SHTS abuse complaint has been investigated

The police have defended their handling of sexual abuse allegations involving the headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), Zakaria Abdulai, amid questions over why investigations have so far focused on one complaint despite at least 19 girls reportedly alleging abuse.

MyJoyOnline Education

The police have defended their handling of sexual abuse allegations involving the headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), Zakaria Abdulai, amid questions over why investigations have so far focused on one complaint despite at least 19 girls reportedly alleging abuse. Bekwai Police Public Relations Officer, ASP Lloyd Baidoo, said the police investigate complaints brought to their attention and could pursue additional allegations if other victims reported them or relevant information was formally submitted. Speaking on JoyNews’ Top Story on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, he rejected suggestions that the police were not responding adequately to the growing public concern over the case.
