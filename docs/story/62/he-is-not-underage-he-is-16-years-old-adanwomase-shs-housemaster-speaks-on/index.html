He is not underage; he is 16 years old – Adanwomase SHS housemaster speaks on viral video of first-year student

The Adanwomase SHS authorities have clarified that the viral video of one of their male ‘smallish’ boarding students is not an underage person but a 16-year-old who is qualified to be in school. The authorities said he wrote the BECE with a qualifying grade from WAEC and was posted to the school to study.
