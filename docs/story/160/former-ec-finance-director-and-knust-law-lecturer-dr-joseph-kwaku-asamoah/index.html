A law lecturer at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Dr Joseph Kwaku Asamoah has been appointed Deputy Chairperson of the Electoral Commission (EC). Dr Asamoah has more than 17 years of experience spanning public service, legal practice, academia and electoral governance.
