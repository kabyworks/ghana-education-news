Obuasi SHTS headmaster resigns as NDC constituency secretary, withdraws from chairmanship race

The headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), Zakaria Abdulai, has resigned as Juaben Constituency Secretary of the National Democratic Congress (NDC) and withdrawn his candidature for the constituency chairmanship, amid allegations of sexual abuse involving female students at the school.
