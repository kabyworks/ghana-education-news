TUTAG to consult members and immediately suspend strike after talks with GTEC

The Technical University Teachers’ Association of Ghana (TUTAG) has agreed to consult its general membership on the immediate suspension of its strike following talks with the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC) and Vice-Chancellors of technical universities.
