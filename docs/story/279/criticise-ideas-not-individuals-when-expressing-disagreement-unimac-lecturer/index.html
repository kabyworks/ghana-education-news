Senior Lecturer and Head of the Department of Media Studies at the University of Media, Arts and Communication (UniMAC), Dr Paul Herzuarh, has urged Ghanaians to criticise the views and actions of public officials rather than attack them personally, saying the line between legitimate criticism and insults is crossed...

Senior Lecturer and Head of the Department of Media Studies at the University of Media, Arts and Communication (UniMAC), Dr Paul Herzuarh, has urged Ghanaians to criticise the views and actions of public officials rather than attack them personally, saying the line between legitimate criticism and insults is crossed when comments are intended to demean or dehumanise an individual.
