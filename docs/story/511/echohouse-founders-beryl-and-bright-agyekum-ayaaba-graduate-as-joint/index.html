EchoHouse founders Beryl and Bright Agyekum-Ayaaba graduate as joint valedictorians

Nearly two decades after turning a university magazine into EchoHouse, a marketing agency operating in eight countries, married founders Beryl Agyekum-Ayaaba and Bright Mamud-Ayaaba have reached another milestone in their journey together, graduating as joint valedictorians from a Master of Business Creation programme.

Nearly two decades after turning a university magazine into EchoHouse, a marketing agency operating in eight countries, married founders Beryl Agyekum-Ayaaba and Bright Mamud-Ayaaba have reached another milestone in their journey together, graduating as joint valedictorians from a Master of Business Creation programme. Together since 2006 and married for 10 years, the couple has built their business and personal lives in tandem, learning to balance marriage, parenthood, entrepreneurship and academic demands while growing EchoHouse from a campus idea into an international company. For Bright, sharing the honour with his wife was both surreal and deeply personal.
