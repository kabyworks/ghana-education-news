The Ghana Education Service (GES) has recommended that the headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), Abdulai Zakaria, and other staff implicated in a violent campus disturbance be reposted to different institutions or duty stations. The recommendation follows a committee’s investigation into the August 5, 2026, riot during which some students attacked the headmaster and vandalised property at his bungalow. Deputy Director-General of GES in charge of Quality and Access, Dr Issahaque Munawaru, disclosed the recommendation in a radio interview on Joy FM on Thursday, October 15, 2026. He said the committee’s report reached GES headquarters the previous week and that the Director-General had assigned officials to review its findings.
