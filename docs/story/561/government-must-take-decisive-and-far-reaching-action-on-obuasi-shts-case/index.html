Government must take decisive and far-reaching action on Obuasi SHTS case – Methodist Church Ghana

The Methodist Church Ghana has called on the government to take decisive and far-reaching action on allegations of sexual abuse involving 19 female students of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS).

MyJoyOnline Education

The Methodist Church Ghana has called on the government to take decisive and far-reaching action on allegations of sexual abuse involving 19 female students of Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS). The Church is demanding an independent and transparent investigation into the allegations against the school's headmaster, Zakaria Abdulai, as well as accountability for any officials or institutions whose actions or inaction may have contributed to the alleged abuse. The allegations involving Obuasi SHTS have attracted national attention, with the number of students reportedly alleging abuse reaching 19.
