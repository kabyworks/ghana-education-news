GTEC cautions universities against compromising quality in doctoral admissions

The Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC) has cautioned universities against admitting more doctoral candidates than their institutional capacity can adequately support, warning that the practice could undermine the quality and credibility of doctoral education in the country.
