Mfantsipim’s Musical memory gets a new lease of life in 150th Anniversary anthology

For 150 years, Mfantsipim School has built a legacy that extends beyond its classrooms. Its songs and hymns have accompanied generations of students, marked institutional milestones and preserved memories of people who helped shape its history. Now, the Mfantsipim Old Boys Association (MOBA) has brought many of those compositions together in a 14-track collection, the Mfantsipim Sesquicentennial Anthology, launched as part of the school’s 150th anniversary celebrations. The project, recorded and produced by lawyer Ace Anan Ankomah (MOBA ’84) and sound engineer Herbert Ebenezer Crassie, combines new recordings with the reconstruction of historic works, some of which required painstaking research and recollection. Its origins were modest.
