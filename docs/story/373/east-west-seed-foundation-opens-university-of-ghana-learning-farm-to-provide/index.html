East-West Seed Foundation opens University of Ghana Learning Farm to provide hands-on agricultural training

The East-West Seed Knowledge Transfer Foundation has opened a learning farm at the University of Ghana in Accra to provide students and local farmers with practical training in vegetable production and give partners an opportunity to observe its work.
