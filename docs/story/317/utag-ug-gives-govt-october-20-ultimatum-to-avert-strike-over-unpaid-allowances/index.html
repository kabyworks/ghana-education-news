UTAG-UG gives gov’t October 20 ultimatum to avert strike over unpaid allowances

The University of Ghana branch of the University Teachers Association of Ghana (UTAG-UG) has given the government until Tuesday, October 20, 2026, to demonstrate its commitment to paying lecturers’ Book and Research Allowances (BRA) or risk a possible strike.
