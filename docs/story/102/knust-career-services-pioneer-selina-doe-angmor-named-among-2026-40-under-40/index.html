KNUST Career Services pioneer Selina Doe Angmor named among 2026 40 Under 40 honourees

Mrs Selina Doe Angmor, pioneer and first Director of the KNUST Career Services Centre, has been recognised among the 2026 40 Under 40 honourees in the Human Resources category. She received the recognition at a ceremony held at the Kempinski Hotel in Accra on September 12, 2026.

Mrs Selina Doe Angmor, pioneer and first Director of the KNUST Career Services Centre, has been recognised among the 2026 40 Under 40 honourees in the Human Resources category. She received the recognition at a ceremony held at the Kempinski Hotel in Accra on September 12, 2026. The award recognises her work in human resources, higher education, workforce development and career services, particularly her contribution to connecting students and graduates with opportunities in the world of work.
