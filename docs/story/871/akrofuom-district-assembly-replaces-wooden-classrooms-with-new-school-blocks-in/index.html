Akrofuom District Assembly replaces wooden classrooms with new school blocks in two communities

The Akrofuom District Assembly has built two classroom blocks to replace the wooden structures pupils and students were using in two communities in the district. The projects are a six-unit classroom block for the Islamic Institute at Gyaasihenekrom and a three-unit block at Yawowusukrom.
