MAA and KSrelief sign Joint Implementation Programme for 20 solar-powered boreholes in Ghana Markaz Aleawn Alyaqin Humanitarian Services (MAA) and the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre (KSrelief) have signed a Joint Implementation Programme (JIP) for the construction of 20 solar-powered boreholes across five regions of Ghana. The JIP was signed at the KSrelief headquarters in Riyadh and will be implemented over a period of eight months, benefiting an estimated 76,555 people in schools and communities. The beneficiary locations include Asawase M/A Cluster of Schools, Sepe Cluster of Schools, Akrom Cluster of Schools, Garden City Special School, Sakafia Islamic Senior High School, Parkoso Senior High School, Kumasi Academy, New Konkrompe Senior High School and the University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR).
