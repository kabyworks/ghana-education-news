University Basic School, Legon, wins CSIR quiz for third consecutive year

The University Basic School (UBS), Legon, has won the 2026 Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)-Food Research Institute Quiz Competition, securing its third consecutive title with 70 points.

The University Basic School (UBS), Legon, has won the 2026 Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)-Food Research Institute Quiz Competition, securing its third consecutive title with 70 points. UBS finished ahead of Christ the King International School, which scored 63 points, The Ridge Church School with 50 points, and Armed Forces Basic School with 47 points. The competition was held at the CSIR-Food Research Institute's conference hall at Shiashie in Accra as part of activities to commemorate World Food Day, observed annually on October 16. The latest victory extends UBS's winning streak since it first participated in the competition in 2024, having also emerged champion in the 2024 and 2025 editions.
