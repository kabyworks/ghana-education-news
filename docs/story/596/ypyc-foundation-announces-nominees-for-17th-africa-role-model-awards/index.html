YPYC Foundation announces nominees for 17th Africa Role Model Awards

The Young Professionals and Youth Coalition (YPYC) Foundation has announced its shortlist for the 17th Africa Role Model Awards, with several prominent personalities from Ghana and other African countries nominated for recognition.

MyJoyOnline Education

The Young Professionals and Youth Coalition (YPYC) Foundation has announced its shortlist for the 17th Africa Role Model Awards, with several prominent personalities from Ghana and other African countries nominated for recognition. The winners will be announced at the 17th Transform Summit and Africa Role Model Awards ceremony scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026, at the ISSER Conference Hall of the University of Ghana, Legon. The event, which begins at 12:00 noon, will be held under the theme, “Leadership in Action: Breaking Barriers and Building Success.” Chief of Staff, Dr Julius Debrah, is expected to attend as the Special Guest of Honour. The awards seek to recognise individuals whose contributions have made an impact in areas including traditional leadership, academia, religion, business, media, engineering, politics, education, law, energy and philanthropy.
