Jimmy Aglah’s ‘When the River Testified’ approved by NaCCA as Supplementary Reader

Ghanaian author and media executive Jimmy Aglah has reached a major milestone in his literary career following the approval of his novel, When the River Testified, by the National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA) as a supplementary reader for Ghana’s pre-tertiary education system.

Ghanaian author and media executive Jimmy Aglah has reached a major milestone in his literary career following the approval of his novel, When the River Testified, by the National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA) as a supplementary reader for Ghana’s pre-tertiary education system. The approval opens the way for the book to be used as supplementary reading material within Ghana’s education system and positions the novel for wider circulation among schools, teachers and students.
