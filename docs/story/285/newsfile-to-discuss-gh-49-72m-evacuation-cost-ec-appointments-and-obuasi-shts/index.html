Newsfile to discuss GH¢49.72m evacuation cost, EC appointments and Obuasi SHTS abuse case

JoyNews’ flagship current affairs programme, Newsfile, will on Saturday, October 17, examine four major national issues that have dominated public debate over the past week.

MyJoyOnline Education

JoyNews’ flagship current affairs programme, Newsfile, will on Saturday, October 17, examine four major national issues that have dominated public debate over the past week. Hosted by Samson Lardy Anyenini, the programme will discuss the GH¢49.72 million spent evacuating Ghanaians from South Africa, controversy over appointments to the Electoral Commission (EC), allegations of sexual abuse at Obuasi Senior High Technical School (SHTS), and the dismissal of a GH¢15 million defamation suit filed by Minority Leader Alexander Afenyo-Markin. The programme airs from 8:00am to 12:00 noon on Joy 99.7 FM, JoyNews and other platforms.
