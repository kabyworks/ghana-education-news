Generation Ghana targets 50,000 jobs for young people in five years

Generation Ghana is targeting 50,000 jobs for young people over the next five years as it expands efforts to equip graduates with digital skills and connect them to employment opportunities.

MyJoyOnline Education

Generation Ghana is targeting 50,000 jobs for young people over the next five years as it expands efforts to equip graduates with digital skills and connect them to employment opportunities. The organisation says only one in 10 university graduates secures what it describes as dignified and fulfilling employment within the first year after graduation, a situation it hopes to address through its Ghana Remote Online Work (GROW) initiative. Country Programme Director of Generation Ghana, Dr Vandyck Lomotey, said the organisation was determined to improve the employment prospects of young people by equipping them with skills that match the demands of the job market. “Currently, as we speak now, it is only one person out of 10 graduates that will get a job that is dignified and fulfilling within the first year of graduating from the university. That is the narrative we would like to change,” he said.
