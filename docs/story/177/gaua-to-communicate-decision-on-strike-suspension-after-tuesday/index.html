GAUA to communicate decision on strike suspension after Tuesday

The Ghana Association of University Administrators (GAUA) says it will consult its members on the outcome of talks with the Education Ministry before deciding whether to suspend its two-month-long strike.

MyJoyOnline Education

The Ghana Association of University Administrators (GAUA) says it will consult its members on the outcome of talks with the Education Ministry before deciding whether to suspend its two-month-long strike. The association is expected to communicate its decision after Tuesday, October 13, 2026, although it has not announced a specific date for the consultation. The position follows a meeting on Monday at the Ministry of Education in Accra involving GAUA leadership, the Technical University Senior Administrators Association of Ghana (TUSAAG), the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC) and the Education Minister, Haruna Iddrisu.
